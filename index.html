What Is Loyverse WooCommerce Integration?

Loyverse is a widely used cloud-based Point of Sale (POS) system designed for retail stores, cafes, and small businesses. WooCommerce is a flexible eCommerce platform built on WordPress that allows businesses to sell products online with ease.

SKUPlugs acts as the bridge between these two platforms. It automatically syncs products, inventory levels, orders, and customer information between Loyverse and WooCommerce. This eliminates the need for manual updates and ensures that both your offline and online stores function as one unified system.

Key Features of SKUPlugs Loyverse WooCommerce Integration

1. Real-Time Inventory Synchronization

SKUPlugs ensures that inventory is automatically updated in both Loyverse and WooCommerce whenever a sale occurs. Whether a product is sold in-store or online, your inventory remains consistent and reliable.

2. Automatic Product Sync

All WooCommerce orders are automatically pushed into Loyverse POS, enabling retailers to manage and track orders from both online and offline channels efficiently.

5. Secure and Automated Data Transfer
